# Momowo 摸摸我 User Guide

<a id="languages"></a>

**English** · [简体中文](#zh-hans) · [繁體中文](#zh-hant) · [Bahasa Indonesia](#indonesian) · [Deutsch](#german) · [Français](#french) · [日本語](#japanese) · [Español](#spanish)

This guide is for using a finished Momowo. To build one yourself, see the **Momowo Build Guide**.

<a id="english"></a>

Momowo ("pat me") is a little desk friend that helps you relax. Its eyes come alive when you pat it, and it keeps you company through the day. It shows the time, your calendars and festivals, prayer times, the weather and air quality, the news read by a character you pick, and a quote for the moment. It also has a focus timer with a breathing exercise, and you can play games with it from your phone.

Momowo speaks English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Indonesian, German, French, Japanese and Spanish.

You don't need any technical knowledge to use Momowo. Everything is set up from your phone.

---

## 1. Getting to know Momowo

- **The screen** on the front shows Momowo's eyes and its pages.
- **The touch spot** is on top of Momowo's head. It is the only button. You don't need to press hard, because Momowo feels your finger through the case.
- **The USB-C socket** on one side is for power.
- **The little slits** on the other side let you hear Momowo's sounds.
- **Momowo can feel movement.** It knows when you pat the case, shake it, tilt it or drop it.

---

## 2. Switching it on

1. Plug Momowo into a USB-C phone charger (or a computer) with a USB-C cable.
2. Momowo wakes up, shows its logo, then opens its eyes.

That's it. There is no on/off switch: Momowo is on whenever it has power. If you unplug it, it remembers all your settings.

Momowo already works without Wi-Fi: you get the eyes, the clock, the calendars, the built-in quotes and the focus timer. Connect it to your Wi-Fi to add the weather, the news, public holidays and the AI features.

---

## 3. Setting it up from your phone

You only need your phone's camera. There is no app to install.

1. **Hold your finger on Momowo's head for 6 seconds.** After 4 seconds it tells you to keep holding. At 6 seconds a square code (a QR code) appears on the screen.
2. **Point your phone's camera at the code** and tap the link or notice that pops up. Your phone connects to Momowo, and the settings page opens by itself.
   - If the page doesn't open, scan the code again. You can also open your phone's web browser and type `192.168.4.1`.
3. As soon as the page opens, Momowo takes the time and your time zone from your phone, even with no internet.
4. Choose your language first. The page changes to that language straight away.
5. Fill in as much or as little as you like (see the list below), then tap **Save**. Momowo restarts with your settings.

The setup screen closes by itself after 10 minutes if no phone is connected. To close it sooner, hold your finger on Momowo's head for about a second.

**Opening the settings page again later:** once Momowo is on your Wi-Fi, any phone or computer on the same Wi-Fi can open the page. The address looks like `momowo-a1b2.local` (the letters and numbers are different for every Momowo). It appears on Momowo's screen after you save, and under the eyes for the first 10 minutes after Momowo gets online. Type it into your web browser. Holding the touch spot for 6 seconds also always works.

### What you can set

| Section | What it's for |
|---|---|
| Language | The language Momowo uses for everything it shows and says. |
| Wi-Fi | Your home Wi-Fi name and password. You can add a second one, such as your phone's hotspot, for when home Wi-Fi isn't around. Momowo can only use 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi (most home routers offer it; see *Troubleshooting* if yours doesn't show up). |
| About you | Your name, so Momowo can greet you. You can also say whether you are male, female or other / non-binary, so the AI uses the right words in languages like French and Spanish (this is optional). Your hobbies let Momowo's thoughts, and sometimes the news, mention things you like. |
| Where you are | Leave it on automatic, or type your suburb or town and country. This sets the weather, sunrise and sunset, prayer times and which country's public holidays you see. |
| Clock & bedtime | 12 or 24 hour time, and when you go to sleep and wake up. At night Momowo dims its screen and stays quiet. |
| Calendars | The normal calendar is always on. You can add any of these: Chinese lunar, Islamic (Hijri), Buddhist, Hindu and Jewish (Hebrew). If you turn on the Islamic calendar, you can also choose how prayer times are calculated, a later Asr time (Hanafi), a chime at each prayer, and a one-day adjustment to match your local moon sighting. |
| Focus timer | How long you focus (15 to 90 minutes) and how long your breaks are (3 to 20 minutes). |
| Motion | Turn Momowo's reactions to movement on or off, and choose how sensitive it is. If its eyes look the wrong way when you tilt it, tick *My eyes look the wrong way when tilted*. |
| Sounds | Sound on or off, the volume, a click when you touch it, and a beep on the hour. |
| News | Where the news comes from. Sources are grouped by language, and include Chinese sources from China, Singapore, Malaysia, Taiwan and Hong Kong, and Indonesian sources such as Kompas, ANTARA, detikNews and CNN Indonesia. You can also have the headlines translated into your language, choose a one-line summary or a short bulletin, and pick the character who reads you the news. |
| Quotes | Tick any mix: ancient China, ancient Greece, modern thinkers, the Bible, the Quran, the Torah, the Dhammapada (the Buddha's sayings) and famous people you choose (type their names, for example *Einstein, Marie Curie*). Then choose whether the AI translates each quote or explains what it means for your day. |
| AI | Which AI service Momowo uses, its key, the model, how often it shares a new feeling (every 30 minutes to once a day, or never) and a daily limit (see section 9). |

## 4. Using the touch spot

| What you do | What happens |
|---|---|
| Tap | Go to the next page. |
| Tap twice quickly | Go back to the eyes. Momowo is happy to see you. |
| Hold for about 1 second | Do something on the page you're on (see below). |
| Hold for 2 seconds | On the focus page: start a breathing exercise, or reset the timer. |
| Hold for 6 seconds | Open phone setup (the square code). |

**Holding for about 1 second on each page:**

- **Eyes:** gives Momowo a pat (hearts!).
- **Clock:** switches between 12 and 24 hour time.
- **Calendar:** with the Islamic calendar on, moves the Hijri date by a day (0, +1, −1) to match your local moon sighting.
- **Weather** and **News:** checks for the latest.
- **Quote:** shows a new quote.
- **Focus:** starts, pauses or carries on the timer.

---

## 5. Moving Momowo

| What you do | What Momowo does |
|---|---|
| Pat or tap the case | Counts it as a pat when its eyes are showing. |
| Shake it | On the quote page you get a new quote. Anywhere else Momowo gets dizzy, with spiral eyes. |
| Tilt it | Its eyes look the way it leans. If it stays tipped over for a couple of seconds, it asks you to stand it up straight. |
| Drop it | Momowo gets a fright and says so. (Please try not to!) |

Momowo learns how it normally stands a moment after it's switched on, and again whenever you move it somewhere new and leave it for a minute. If it reacts to bumps on your desk, choose a lower sensitivity on the settings page. If it misses your pats, choose a higher one.

## 6. The pages

Tap the touch spot to go through them in this order. After 90 seconds Momowo goes back to its eyes by itself.

### Eyes

Momowo's eyes blink, look around and show how it feels: happy, sleepy, surprised or dizzy. Hearts float up when you pat it, and lots of pats get a thank-you.

A line of text along the bottom shows a greeting, Momowo's thought of the moment, the weather, the time left on your focus timer, and (with the Islamic calendar on) the next prayer.

To keep the screen looking good for years, the face moves a little every few minutes, and the screen dims when nobody has touched Momowo for 20 minutes. A touch brightens it again.

### Clock

A big clock with the date. Every few seconds it shows where you are and your time zone. Summer time (daylight saving) changes by itself, even without Wi-Fi.

With the Islamic calendar on, the clock takes turns with the six prayer times, each with its own small picture:

| Prayer | Picture |
|---|---|
| Fajr | a crescent moon above the horizon |
| Sunrise | half a sun with an arrow pointing up |
| Dhuhr | a full sun |
| Asr | a low sun and a stick with a long shadow |
| Maghrib | half a sun with an arrow pointing down |
| Isha | a crescent moon with a star |

The next prayer is highlighted. During Ramadan, a third view shows the fasting times (imsak and iftar), the day of Ramadan and a countdown to iftar.

### Calendar

First a month view, with today in a filled box and public holidays in an outlined box. Then today's public holiday, or the next one if it's a week away or less (for example *"Deepavali in 5 days"*). After that, one line for each calendar you turned on:

- **Chinese:** the lunar date, the 24 solar terms and festivals.
- **Islamic:** the Hijri date, plus New Year, Mawlid, Isra Mi'raj, the start of Ramadan, Eid al-Fitr, Arafah and Eid al-Adha.
- **Buddhist:** the Buddhist year, plus Vesak, Guanyin's days, the Buddha's birthday, Ullambana, Bodhi Day and uposatha days.
- **Jewish:** the Hebrew date, which changes at sunset. Festivals include Rosh Hashanah, Yom Kippur, Sukkot, Hanukkah, Purim, Passover and Shavuot (with the Israeli festival days in Israel). On Fridays it shows the Shabbat candle-lighting time, and on Saturdays it wishes you Shabbat shalom.
- **Hindu:** the month and lunar day, plus Makar Sankranti (Pongal), Thaipusam, Maha Shivaratri, Holi, Ugadi, Ram Navami, Raksha Bandhan, Janmashtami, Ganesh Chaturthi, Navaratri, Dussehra and Diwali (Deepavali).

On festival days and public holidays, Momowo greets you on its eyes page. Public holidays are the nationwide ones for the country you're in, and need Wi-Fi to download once a year.

### Weather

The views take turns: the weather now, the air quality, tomorrow, the next 3 days, then sunrise and sunset.

**Air quality** is shown as a number from 0 to 500 with a word for how good it is: Good, Moderate, Unhealthy for sensitive groups, Unhealthy, Very unhealthy or Hazardous. When the air is unhealthy, Momowo reminds you to go easy outside. It feels the air too: its eyes turn tired or sad, and it gives a little cough each time the air gets worse.

### News

First the AI reporter's summary, read in your chosen character's voice, then the headlines. If you turned on translation, the headlines appear in your language. Now and then, if a story connects to your hobbies, the reporter mentions it.

You can choose one of the built-in characters, or type your own. Momowo looks up how your character talks, so they sound like themselves. If you choose a real person who did terrible harm, the news is read in a calm, neutral voice instead.

### Quote

Each quote is shown in its original language for 10 seconds (for example Greek, Hebrew, Arabic or classical Chinese; the Bible is in English), then a translation or explanation for 10 seconds. Hebrew and Arabic read right to left, and long verses scroll slowly.

Quotes suit the moment: rainy days bring comfort and patience, sunny mornings bring hope, nights bring rest, and autumn brings change. A new quote comes when the weather or time of day changes. Shake Momowo, or hold the touch spot, for a new one any time.

When a quote is already in your language, the AI explains what it means instead of translating it. Translations and explanations need the AI (section 9); without it you see an English translation.

For **famous people**, the AI picks a real quote by someone on your list. It is careful not to invent quotes, but it can still make mistakes, so treat an unusual one with a little doubt.

### Focus

A focus timer (also called a Pomodoro timer). Hold to start. When your focus time ends, Momowo starts your break, and every fourth break is longer. During a break, a breathing circle guides you: breathe in for 4 seconds, hold for 4, and breathe out for 6. When the timer isn't running, hold for 2 seconds to do the breathing exercise on its own.

## 7. Playing games

Play on your phone while Momowo plays along on its screen. Your phone needs to be on the same Wi-Fi as Momowo.

1. Open the settings page (see section 3).
2. Tap **🎮 Play a game with me** at the top.
3. Pick a game. Momowo's screen switches to the game, and it reacts to every move: smiling eyes when it wins, a surprised face when you do.

| Game | How to play |
|---|---|
| ✊ Rock paper scissors | Pick on your phone. Momowo shows both hands and who won. |
| ⭕ Tic-tac-toe | You are ✕ and Momowo is ◯. It plays fairly, and doesn't always block you. |
| 🔢 Guess my number | Momowo thinks of a number from 1 to 100. Guess, and it tells you higher or lower. |
| 🧠 Memory | Watch the arrows light up on Momowo's screen, each with its own note, then tap them on your phone in the same order. Each round adds one more. |
| 🌟 Star roll | Press Start, pick Momowo up and tilt it to roll the ball onto the stars. You have 30 seconds. From 3 stars a rain cloud drifts about, and bumping into it costs a star. However you're holding Momowo when the countdown ends counts as level. |
| 🧩 Riddles | Momowo's AI makes up a riddle. Type your answer; close answers and small typos count. Ask for a hint, or give up to see the answer. Needs the AI. |
| 🗺️ Quest | Press Start, then put your phone down. The AI tells a short adventure on Momowo's screen, one moment at a time. For each moment, do what the picture shows before the bar runs out (see below). You have 3 hearts, and quick answers score double. Without the AI you get a short quest with just the pictures. |
| ⚡ Quick tap | A reaction game on the touch spot. Open it on your phone, then tap Momowo's head to start (or press Start). Wait while Momowo stares; when the whole screen flashes and it beeps, tap its head as fast as you can. Tapping too soon replays the round, and from round 2 Momowo sometimes pretends by opening its eyes wide without the flash. After 5 rounds you get your average time; the lower the better. Double-tap to leave the game. |

**Quest pictures:**

| Picture | What to do |
|---|---|
| a dot with one ring | tap Momowo's head |
| a dot with two rings | hold your finger on its head for about a second |
| an arrow | tilt Momowo that way |
| Momowo with little lines beside it | shake it gently |

Momowo remembers your best scores. During a game, hold the touch spot to give Momowo a pat, and tap it to go back to the normal pages (except in Star roll, Quest and Quick tap, where touching is part of the game; in Quick tap, double-tap to leave). The game view also closes by itself after 2 minutes without a move.

---

## 8. At night

Between the bedtime and wake-up times you set, Momowo gets sleepy: its eyes close, the screen dims, and it stays quiet. After a while the screen switches off completely. Touch it and it wakes up for a moment. In the morning it wakes up with a little sound.

## 9. The AI (optional)

Momowo can use an AI service to make it more personal. Everything else works without it.

**What the AI adds:**

- A short, kind thought now and then, based on the time, the weather, your hobbies, and how many pats, focus sessions and breaths you've done today.
- The news summary in your chosen character's voice, and translated headlines.
- Translations and explanations of quotes, and quotes from your famous people.
- Public holiday names in your language.
- Riddles and Quest adventures.

**Which AI service:** you can choose one of four. They all do the same things in Momowo.

| Service | Where to get a key | Models you can pick on the settings page |
|---|---|---|
| Claude (Anthropic) | [console.anthropic.com](https://console.anthropic.com) | Haiku 5.5 (the default), Sonnet 5.5, Opus 5.5, Fable 5.1 |
| ChatGPT (OpenAI) | [platform.openai.com/api-keys](https://platform.openai.com/api-keys) | GPT-4.1 mini (the default), GPT-4.1 nano, GPT-4.1 |
| Kimi (Moonshot) | [platform.moonshot.ai](https://platform.moonshot.ai) | Kimi K2.6 |
| DeepSeek | [platform.deepseek.com](https://platform.deepseek.com) | DeepSeek Flash (the default), DeepSeek V4 Pro |

**How to turn it on:**

1. On a computer or phone, go to the website of the service you chose (see the table) and create an account. This is a separate account from the service's chat app, even if you already use that.
2. Add some credit in the billing section. You pay for what Momowo uses, a little at a time.
3. Find the **API keys** section, create a new key and copy it. It's a long piece of text. Keep it private, like a password.
4. Open Momowo's settings page and go to the **AI** section.
5. Choose your service under **AI provider**, then paste the key into **AI API key**.
6. Choose a model (the default is a good start), how often Momowo shares a new feeling (every 90 minutes to start with; each one uses one request) and a daily limit.
7. Tap **Save**.

If you change to a different service later, tap **Save** before switching, or the key you just typed is lost. Momowo keeps one saved key for each service.

**What it costs:** a typical day uses a few dozen requests. With Claude's default model this usually costs well under two US dollars a month; each service shows its prices on its website. Bigger models cost more and are slower. With Claude you can also choose how hard it thinks; more thinking also costs more.

**Daily limit:** Momowo stops using the AI for the day once it reaches the limit you choose (25 to 500 requests, 100 to start with). The settings page shows how many it has used today, with a button to start the count again.

## 10. Good to know

- **Prayer times** are calculated using the method of the religious authority in your country (for example MUIS in Singapore, Kemenag in Indonesia and JAKIM in Malaysia). Always follow your local mosque if its times differ. You can choose another method on the settings page.
- **Islamic (Hijri) dates** follow the Umm al-Qura calendar. Local moon sighting can be a day earlier or later. Hold the touch spot on the calendar page to adjust it.
- **Hindu festival dates** are calculated, and can be a day off in some years or places. Please check with your temple.
- **Jewish candle-lighting** is shown 18 minutes before sunset. Some communities light earlier, so follow your own custom.
- **Buddhist festivals** follow the Chinese lunar calendar, so Vesak can differ from the public holiday in some countries.
- **Public holidays** are the official ones for your country. A holiday moved at short notice may not show.
- **Your privacy:** your settings stay on Momowo. Your Wi-Fi password and AI key are never shown again on the settings page.

## 11. Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to try |
|---|---|
| Nothing appears on the screen | Check the cable is pushed in fully and the charger is switched on. Try another cable: some cables only charge and don't power devices well. |
| The square code doesn't appear | Keep your finger still on the very top of Momowo's head for a full 6 seconds. |
| My phone won't open the settings page | Scan the code again. If your phone says the Wi-Fi has "no internet", choose to stay connected. You can also type `192.168.4.1` into your browser. |
| My Wi-Fi isn't in the list, or Momowo can't connect | Momowo only works with 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi. Many routers have two networks; pick the one without "5G" in its name, or ask whoever looks after your Wi-Fi. Check the password. The reason Momowo couldn't connect is shown at the top of the settings page. |
| Momowo is using my backup Wi-Fi | It tries your home Wi-Fi again every 10 minutes and switches back when it can. |
| The clock says "Syncing time…" | Some office or school Wi-Fi blocks the time service. Momowo then takes the time from the next weather or news update. If it still can't, it shows how to set the time from your phone. |
| The time is wrong after a power cut | Open the settings page once, and Momowo takes the time from your phone. |
| The news doesn't load | Hold the touch spot on the News page to try again, or choose another news source. |
| Momowo jumps or gets dizzy when I bump the desk | Choose a lower motion sensitivity on the settings page. |
| The eyes look the wrong way when I tilt Momowo | Tick *My eyes look the wrong way when tilted* in the Motion section. |
| The AI says the daily limit is reached | Raise the limit, or press the button to start today's count again, on the settings page. |
| The AI doesn't answer | Check that the key belongs to the service chosen under *AI provider*, and that your account there still has credit. |
| Prayer times are a few minutes off from my mosque | Try another calculation method, or turn the Hanafi Asr option on or off. |
| I want to start over | Tap **Forget all settings** at the bottom of the settings page. |

## 12. Taking care of Momowo

- Keep it dry and away from direct sun and heat.
- Use a normal USB-C phone charger.
- Clean the screen gently with a soft, dry cloth.

## 简体中文 · 使用说明

本说明适用于已经组装好的 Momowo，不需要任何电子知识。完整内容见上方英文版。

### 1. 开始使用

- **屏幕**在正面，**触摸点**在 Momowo 头顶（唯一的按钮，轻轻一碰即可），侧面的 **USB-C 接口**用来供电，另一侧的小缝是喇叭。
- 用 USB-C 线把 Momowo 接到手机充电器或电脑上，它就会醒来。没有开关，断电后设置也会保留。
- 不连 Wi-Fi 也能用：眼睛、时钟、日历、内置名言和专注计时。连上 Wi-Fi 后还有天气、新闻、节假日和 AI 功能。

### 2. 用手机设置（不用装 App）

1. 用手指按住 Momowo 头顶 **6 秒**，屏幕会出现一个二维码。
2. 用手机相机扫描，点弹出的提示。手机会连上 Momowo，设置页会自动打开。打不开时再扫一次，或在浏览器输入 `192.168.4.1`。
3. 先选择语言，页面会马上变成该语言。Momowo 会从手机获取时间和时区。
4. 按需要填写：Wi-Fi（只支持 2.4 GHz）、名字、爱好、所在地、作息时间、日历、声音、新闻、名言和 AI。
5. 点击 **保存**，Momowo 会重启。

以后在同一 Wi-Fi 下，可在浏览器打开 Momowo 屏幕上显示的地址（例如 `momowo-a1b2.local`）。随时按住头顶 6 秒也可以。

### 3. 触摸和动作

| 操作 | 作用 |
|---|---|
| 轻触 | 下一页 |
| 快速点两下 | 回到眼睛页 |
| 按住约 1 秒 | 当前页的功能：眼睛页是拍拍它，时钟页切换 12/24 小时，天气和新闻页刷新，名言页换一句，专注页开始或暂停 |
| 在专注页按住 2 秒 | 开始呼吸练习，或重置计时 |
| 按住 6 秒 | 打开手机设置 |
| 拍拍外壳 / 摇一摇 / 倾斜 | 拍拍会有爱心；摇一摇会头晕（名言页会换一句）；倾斜时眼睛跟着看过去 |

### 4. 各个页面

依次是：**眼睛**（心情、问候和小提示）、**时钟**（开启伊斯兰历时轮流显示礼拜时间）、**日历**（农历、伊斯兰历、佛历、印度历、犹太历及节假日）、**天气**（含空气质量）、**新闻**（由你选的角色播报）、**名言**（原文显示，再显示翻译或解释）和**专注计时**（含呼吸练习）。90 秒后会自动回到眼睛页。为了保护屏幕，脸会不时轻微移动，20 分钟没人碰时屏幕会变暗。夜里它会睡觉、变暗并保持安静。

### 5. 小游戏

手机和 Momowo 连同一个 Wi-Fi，打开设置页，点顶部的 **🎮 和我玩个游戏**。有猜拳、井字棋、猜数字、记忆、星星滚球（拿起来倾斜）、AI 谜语、冒险和快手拍拍。冒险里按屏幕上的图做：一个圈就轻触头顶，两个圈就按住约 1 秒，箭头就往那边倾斜，带线条的 Momowo 就轻轻摇一摇。快手拍拍：屏幕一闪、发出哔声时，尽快轻触头顶；太早就重来这一轮，5 轮后看平均时间。点两下离开。

### 6. AI（可选）

在设置页的 **AI** 区域选择服务（Claude、ChatGPT、Kimi 或 DeepSeek），贴上从该服务网站获取的 **API 密钥**，选模型和每日上限，再点保存。密钥要在该服务的开发者网站（例如 console.anthropic.com、platform.openai.com、platform.moonshot.ai、platform.deepseek.com）注册、充值后创建，像密码一样保管好。费用按用量计算，默认设置下通常很低。切换服务前请先保存。

### 7. 常见问题

| 问题 | 解决办法 |
|---|---|
| 屏幕不亮 | 检查线是否插好，换一条线或充电器试试 |
| 找不到我的 Wi-Fi | Momowo 只支持 2.4 GHz，请选名字里没有 “5G” 的那个网络 |
| 显示“正在同步时间” | 有些公司或学校网络会屏蔽对时服务；稍等，或打开一次设置页从手机对时 |
| 停电后时间不对 | 打开一次设置页即可 |
| AI 没反应 | 确认密钥属于所选的服务，账户里还有余额，且没到每日上限 |

1. Momowo の頭に指を **6 秒**のせたままにすると、QR コードが出ます。
2. スマホのカメラで読み取り、出てきた通知をタップします。スマホが Momowo につながり、設定ページが開きます。開かないときはもう一度読み取るか、ブラウザで `192.168.4.1` を開きます。
3. まず言語を選ぶと、ページがすぐその言語になります。時刻とタイムゾーンはスマホから取り込まれます。
4. 必要なところを入力します：Wi-Fi（2.4 GHz のみ）、名前、趣味、場所、寝る時間、カレンダー、音、ニュース、名言、AI。
5. **保存**をタップすると Momowo が再起動します。

あとから同じ Wi-Fi で、Momowo の画面に出るアドレス（例：`momowo-a1b2.local`）をブラウザで開けます。6 秒長押しはいつでも使えます。

### 3. さわる・動かす

| すること | どうなるか |
|---|---|
| タップ | 次のページ |
| すばやく 2 回タップ | 目の画面にもどる |
| 約 1 秒押す | そのページの操作：なでる、12/24 時間表示の切り替え、天気やニュースの更新、新しい名言、集中タイマーの開始・一時停止 |
| 集中ページで 2 秒押す | 呼吸練習、またはタイマーのリセット |
| 6 秒押す | スマホ設定を開く |
| ケースをたたく・振る・傾ける | たたくとハート、振ると目が回る（名言ページでは新しい名言）、傾けるとその向きを見る |

### 4. ページ

順番に：**目**（気分、あいさつ、ひとこと）、**時計**（イスラム暦をオンにすると礼拝時刻も）、**カレンダー**（旧暦、イスラム暦、仏暦、ヒンドゥー暦、ユダヤ暦と祝日）、**天気**（空気の質つき）、**ニュース**（選んだキャラクターが読み上げ）、**名言**（原文のあとに訳か解説）、**集中**（呼吸ガイドつき）。90 秒たつと目の画面にもどります。画面を長持ちさせるため、顔はときどき少し動き、20 分さわらないと画面が暗くなります。夜は眠って暗くなり、静かにしています。

### 5. ゲーム

スマホを同じ Wi-Fi につなぎ、設定ページ上の **🎮 いっしょにゲームしよう** をタップします。じゃんけん、三目並べ、数当て、記憶ゲーム、星ころがし（持ち上げて傾ける）、AI なぞなぞ、冒険、はやタッチがあります。冒険では絵のとおりに：輪が 1 つ＝頭をタップ、輪が 2 つ＝約 1 秒押す、矢印＝その向きに傾ける、線のついた Momowo＝やさしく振る。はやタッチでは、画面が光ってピッと鳴ったらすぐ頭をタップ。早すぎるとその回はやり直しで、5 回の平均タイムが出ます。2 回タップで終了。

### 6. AI（任意）

設定ページの **AI** で、サービス（Claude、ChatGPT、Kimi、DeepSeek）を選び、そのサービスの **API キー**を貼り付け、モデルと 1 日の上限を選んで保存します。キーは各サービスの開発者サイト（例：console.anthropic.com、platform.openai.com、platform.moonshot.ai、platform.deepseek.com）で登録し、クレジットを入れてから作成します。パスワードと同じように大切に保管してください。料金は使った分だけで、初期設定ならふつうはわずかです。サービスを切り替える前に保存してください。

### 7. 困ったとき

| 症状 | ためすこと |
|---|---|
| 画面がつかない | ケーブルを奥まで差し込む。別のケーブルや充電器でためす |
| 自分の Wi-Fi が出てこない | Momowo は 2.4 GHz のみ。名前に「5G」がないほうを選ぶ |
| 「時刻を同期中」のまま | 会社や学校のネットワークでは時刻サービスがふさがれていることがあります。待つか、設定ページを一度開く |
| 停電のあと時刻がずれる | 設定ページを一度開く |
| AI が答えない | キーが選んだサービスのものか、残高があるか、1 日の上限に達していないかを確認 |
| 礼拝時刻がモスクと数分ちがう | 別の計算方法をためすか、ハナフィー派のアスルを切り替える |

## Español · Instrucciones de uso

Esta guía es para un Momowo ya montado. No hace falta saber electrónica. Todos los detalles están en la versión en inglés de arriba.

### 1. Para empezar

- La **pantalla** está delante y el **punto táctil** en lo alto de la cabeza (el único botón; basta con rozarlo). La **toma USB-C** del lateral es para la corriente, y las ranuras del otro lado dejan salir el sonido.
- Conecta Momowo a un cargador de móvil o a un ordenador con un cable USB-C y se despierta. No tiene interruptor, y los ajustes se guardan aunque lo desenchufes.
- Sin wifi ya tienes los ojos, el reloj, los calendarios, las frases incluidas y el temporizador de concentración. Con wifi se añaden el tiempo, las noticias, los festivos y la IA.

### 2. Configuración con el móvil (sin app)

1. Deja el dedo **6 segundos** sobre la cabeza de Momowo hasta que aparezca un código QR.
2. Escanéalo con la cámara del móvil y toca el aviso. El móvil se conecta a Momowo y se abre la página de ajustes. Si no, vuelve a escanear o escribe `192.168.4.1` en el navegador.
3. Elige primero el idioma; la página cambia al momento. Momowo toma la hora y la zona horaria del móvil.
4. Rellena lo que quieras: wifi (solo 2,4 GHz), nombre, aficiones, lugar, horario de sueño, calendarios, sonidos, noticias, frases e IA.
5. Toca **Guardar**. Momowo se reinicia.

Más adelante, en la misma wifi, abre en el navegador la dirección que muestra Momowo (por ejemplo `momowo-a1b2.local`). Mantener 6 segundos siempre funciona.

### 3. Tocar y mover

| Lo que haces | Lo que pasa |
|---|---|
| Tocar | Página siguiente |
| Dos toques rápidos | Volver a los ojos |
| Mantener 1 segundo aprox. | Acción de la página: acariciar a Momowo, 12/24 h, actualizar el tiempo o las noticias, nueva frase, iniciar o pausar la concentración |
| Mantener 2 segundos en la página de concentración | Ejercicio de respiración, o reiniciar el temporizador |
| Mantener 6 segundos | Abrir los ajustes en el móvil |
| Dar golpecitos, agitar, inclinar | Los golpecitos dan corazones; agitarlo lo marea (en la página de frases: frase nueva); al inclinarlo mira hacia ese lado |

### 4. Las páginas

En orden: **Ojos** (ánimo, saludos y avisos), **Reloj** (con horarios de oración si el calendario islámico está activado), **Calendario** (chino, islámico, budista, hindú, judío y festivos), **Tiempo** (con calidad del aire), **Noticias** (leídas por el personaje que elijas), **Frase** (en su idioma original y luego traducción o explicación) y **Concentración** (con respiración guiada). A los 90 segundos vuelve a los ojos. Para cuidar la pantalla, la cara se mueve un poco de vez en cuando y la pantalla se atenúa tras 20 minutos sin tocarlo. De noche Momowo duerme, se atenúa y se queda en silencio.

### 5. Juegos

Conecta el móvil a la misma wifi, abre la página de ajustes y toca **🎮 Juega conmigo** arriba. Hay piedra, papel o tijera, tres en raya, adivina el número, memoria, rueda estrellas (levántalo e inclínalo), acertijos con IA, la Aventura y Toque rápido. En la Aventura sigue el dibujo: un anillo = tocar la cabeza, dos anillos = mantener 1 segundo, flecha = inclinarlo hacia ese lado, Momowo con rayitas = agitarlo suavemente. En Toque rápido, cuando la pantalla parpadee y pite, toca la cabeza lo antes posible; si tocas antes, la ronda se repite. Tras 5 rondas verás tu media. Toca dos veces para salir.

### 6. IA (opcional)

En la sección **IA** de la página de ajustes, elige un servicio (Claude, ChatGPT, Kimi o DeepSeek), pega la **clave API** de ese servicio, elige el modelo y el límite diario y guarda. La clave se crea en la web para desarrolladores del servicio (por ejemplo console.anthropic.com, platform.openai.com, platform.moonshot.ai, platform.deepseek.com) tras registrarte y añadir saldo. Guárdala como una contraseña. Se paga según el uso, y con los ajustes por defecto suele ser poco. Guarda antes de cambiar de servicio.

### 7. Si algo falla

| Problema | Prueba esto |
|---|---|
| La pantalla no se enciende | Mete bien el cable; prueba otro cable u otro cargador |
| No aparece mi wifi | Momowo solo funciona con 2,4 GHz; elige la red sin «5G» en el nombre |
| «Sincronizando la hora» | Algunas redes de oficina o escuela bloquean el servicio de hora; espera o abre una vez la página de ajustes |
| Hora incorrecta tras un apagón | Abre una vez la página de ajustes |
| La IA no responde | Comprueba que la clave es del servicio elegido, que queda saldo y que no se ha llegado al límite diario |
| Las horas de oración difieren unos minutos | Prueba otro método, o cambia la opción de Asr hanafí |
